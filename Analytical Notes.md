# How This Analysis Was Approached

This will explain how the Hudson’s Bay analysis was structured from a Business Analyst perspective.

It does not describe a named framework.  
Instead, it outlines how decisions were supported under uncertainty, limited data, and time constraints.

---

## Defining the Real Question

The analysis did not begin with “Why did Hudson’s Bay fail?”

It was reframed as:

“When did the business lose the ability to recover, and how could that have been identified earlier?”

This shifted the focus from explanation to decision timing.

---

## Setting Analytical Scope

The scope was intentionally limited to indicators that:
- leadership could realistically monitor  
- materially affected strategic options  
- changed meaningfully before failure became irreversible  

As a result, the analysis focused on:
- financial sustainability  
- operational efficiency  
- customer behavior  
- digital channel performance  

---

## Working With Imperfect Information

The analysis combined:
- public financial disclosures  
- industry benchmarks  
- observable peer trends  
- analyst assumptions where direct data was unavailable  

Emphasis was placed on direction and rate of change rather than false precision.

---

## Turning Metrics Into Signals

KPIs were evaluated for:
- consistency over time  
- interaction with other metrics  
- impact on future optionality  

Revenue decline alone was not treated as fatal.  
Margin erosion combined with rising leverage was.

---

## Assessing Viability

Rather than assuming failure was inevitable, the analysis tested whether:
- earlier capital reallocation  
- faster execution  
- clearer prioritization  

could have altered outcomes.

The focus was on whether recovery remained economically rational within a shrinking time window.

---

## What This Demonstrates

This work demonstrates the ability to:
- structure ambiguous problems  
- identify early risk signals  
- connect data to decisions  
- assess feasibility, not just ideas  

The emphasis is on judgment and timing.
